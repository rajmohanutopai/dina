Open questions

Open

Score-formula version policy. Bumping RANKING_VERSION requires re-emitting cursors (scoreBucket shifts under new weights). For now the cursor version moves in lockstep — but the two are independent in code, so a future maintainer might not realise a ranking bump must also bump the cursor version. Consider coupling them explicitly.
